## Overview

This site is the home base for my writing: polished blogs, working notes, project
writeups, and older Medium pieces I am gradually bringing back under my own
roof.

The design is deliberately plain. The point is to make the writing and the
ideas feel calm, readable, and personal, with a small Mithila-inspired visual
identity instead of a heavy portfolio shell.

## Tech stack

- **Framework**: Astro
- **Styling**: Tailwind CSS v4
- **Content**: MDX + Astro Content Collections
- **Diagrams**: Mermaid
- **Comments**: Giscus (GitHub Discussions)
- **Analytics**: Google Analytics, with Plausible support kept available
- **Hosting**: Netlify

## What I learned

Personal sites become interesting once they stop being a portfolio checklist and
start becoming a writing system.

The important parts here are not flashy components. They are the editorial
structures: notes versus blogs, drafts versus published posts, tag pages, Medium
archive links, comments, analytics, and enough routing discipline that the site
can keep growing without becoming annoying to maintain.
